- **Bullion and Gifting Ecommerce Platform — Screen and Component Specification**
  > Each screen is decomposed as Screen → component/widget → fields or content → actions and validation. India-focused commerce for bullion, gems, rudraksh, gifts and Gold Vault.
  - **Market research and MVP decision**
    - **Competitor patterns observed**
      - **MMTC-PAMP: direct bullion benchmark**
        - **Live prices and product-specific weights/purity**
        - **Search and filters for metal, product type, price and weight**
        - **Delivery estimate and PIN-code checks**
        - **Purity, certification, QR verification and brand credibility**
        - **Gifting-led coin and bar assortment**
      - **Tanishq: branded gifting benchmark**
        - **Gold and silver coins with weight and price filters**
        - **Occasion-led collections and recognizable brand trust**
        - **Product discovery by weight, karatage, metal and collection**
      - **Augmont: broader gold ecosystem benchmark**
        - **Physical coins and jewellery alongside digital gold/silver**
        - **Buyback and investment-adjacent services increase complexity**
      - **Research sources**
        - **MMTC-PAMP shop: https://www.mmtcpamp.com/shop**
        - **Tanishq gold coins: https://www.tanishq.co.in/shop/gold-coin**
        - **Augmont shop: https://augmont.com/shop**
        - **SEBI caution on digital gold, 8 November 2025: https://www.sebi.gov.in/media-and-notifications/press-releases/nov-2025/caution-to-public-regarding-dealing-in-digital-gold-_97676.html**
        - **BIS hallmarking overview: https://www.bis.gov.in/hallmarking-overview/**
        - **CBIC GST invoice rules: https://cbic-gst.gov.in/gst-invoice-rules.html**
    - **Market implications**
      - **Compete on trust and execution, not catalogue breadth**
        - **Make purity, weight, price breakup, rate timestamp, delivery terms and returns easy to understand**
        - **Show authentic product photography and available certificates for each eligible SKU**
        - **Start with a curated range; do not build every category and automation before demand is proven**
      - **Initial customer hypotheses**
        - **Individual buyers seeking trusted gold or silver coins/bars for saving or gifting**
        - **Festival and occasion buyers who value packaging and delivery confidence**
        - **Corporate gifting buyers who need a credible catalogue, budget guidance and a quote**
      - **Proposed first assortment — hypothesis to validate**
        - **Small curated range of gold coins/bars across a few common weights**
        - **Small curated range of silver coins/bars and gifting-ready items**
        - **Rudraksh, coloured stones and gemstones: limited test collection only after supplier quality and certificate processes are clear**
        - **Corporate gifting: enquiry-led pilot with manual quote and fulfilment**
    - **MVP recommendation**
      - **Launch first: physical-product commerce**
        - **Catalogue, product detail, transparent price/tax breakup, stock, checkout, payment, invoice and insured tracked delivery**
        - **Live metal rate and a controlled price-lock/reprice process for bullion**
        - **Basic admin product, pricing, inventory, order, payment, refund, shipment and support operations**
        - **Customer contact, transactional messages, policies and consent records**
      - **Keep manual where possible**
        - **Corporate quotes and approvals**
        - **Product uploads and catalogue merchandising**
        - **Customer support, returns review and exception handling**
        - **Basic reports exported from orders and payments**
      - **Defer until demand is proven**
        - **Gold Vault digital buy/hold/sell**
        - **Loyalty, wallet, referrals, gift cards and advanced promotions**
        - **AI insights, recommendations and marketing automation**
        - **Full personalization production workflow and automated split delivery**
    - **Validation scorecard**
      - **Commercial signals**
        - **Qualified product-page visits and add-to-cart rate**
        - **Checkout completion and paid conversion**
        - **Average order value and product/category mix**
        - **Gross margin after payment, packaging, insurance, shipping and returns**
        - **Customer acquisition cost by channel and repeat purchase intent**
      - **Operational signals**
        - **Rate mismatch and price-lock expiry incidents**
        - **Payment failures, refunds and reconciliation exceptions**
        - **Dispatch SLA, delivery success and damage/loss rate**
        - **Support contacts per order and return/cancellation reasons**
      - **Decision gate**
        - **Expand only when contribution margin, fulfilment reliability and conversion are acceptable**
        - **Validate willingness to buy before investing in Gold Vault, broad catalogue automation or AI features**
    - **Priority colour legend**
      - **Green — MVP required**
        - **Needed to launch and safely test physical-product demand**
          - **Add in store pickup**
      - **Amber — validate next**
        - **Useful if customer behaviour or early operating load justifies it**
      - **Grey — later phase**
        - **Defer until product-market evidence or compliance readiness supports it**
